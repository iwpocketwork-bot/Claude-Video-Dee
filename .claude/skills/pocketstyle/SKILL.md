## Brand rules for every style
- Logo files in `pocket-reel/public/`: `logo-white.png`, `logo-dark.png`, `wordmark-white.png`, `icon-white.png`, `icon-dark.png`. Keep the logo **small**; on end cards show the text first and the logo **last**.
- Colours: black/dark `#0d0e13` / `#1A1D21`, white, lime `#f7fd92` (main accent), orange flame `#ff6b3d` (secondary). Don't add many colours.
- Fonts: standard sans only (Poppins in most styles). **No cursive.** Don't mix many typefaces.
- Never let graphics, text, logo or captions overlap. Check stills of every scene in every orientation.
- Facts: **26 tahun pengalaman** (never 6). Services: Video Production, Mobilegraphy, Photography, Graphic Design, Advertising, Event Coverage, Training Programs. Training is called **"Smartphone Videomaking"**. Kami rakam guna smartphone je. Say "klik" (not "ketik").
- Contact: WhatsApp Dee 011-1620 0864 · @pocket_production · pocketproduction.my.
- Language: Bahasa Melayu santai Pocket, with English words mixed in. Don't translate other people's English videos into BM; keep the original and add overlays with a source credit.
- Formats: 9:16 Reels 1080×1920 and 16:9 website 1920×1080, 30 fps.

## Shared workflow
1. Plan the script/scenes → stills contact sheet (`npx remotion still src/index.ts <Id> f.png --frame=N --scale=0.4`) → fix overlaps.
2. Preview 540p (`--scale=0.5`) → wait for user approval → final 1080p.
3. Render: `npx remotion render src/index.ts <Id> out/raw.mp4 --browser-executable=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium_headless_shell-1243/chrome-headless-shell-linux64/chrome-headless-shell --gl=angle --concurrency=4 --crf=18`
4. Audio master (music-only videos): `-af "aresample=48000,loudnorm=I=-15:TP=-3:LRA=11,aresample=48000,alimiter=limit=0.6:attack=1:release=50:level=false"` → ≈ -14 LUFS, peak ≈ -2 dBFS. VO videos: `alimiter=limit=0.85:attack=2:release=60:level=false,loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=11`.
5. Scenes mount by **global frame** with a `Show from/to` wrapper (not `<Sequence>`), so cues and wipes use absolute frames. Call React hooks before any early `return null`.
6. Voiceover: ElevenLabs voice "Shahrul – Energetic Social Media Creator" (`1uDSnuWUOvbosqTWAzfW`, model `eleven_v4`); word timings from transcription → `words.ts`.
7. Music: `public/music.mp3` (The Mountain) or `public/music_subclair.mp3`; SFX in `public/sfx/` (generated by `tools/sfx_gen.py`).
8. Send the MP4s, add them to `pocket-reel/.gitignore` exceptions, commit and push.
